A. AI-summary transparency (D3 / VariantA) — currently the operator sees a polished AI draft but no easy way to verify "did it actually use my SOP answers + that GPS evidence?" D3's side-by-side Input/Output and VariantA's expandable per-leg SOP transcript both solve this. Pick one — I lean D3 because it doubles as proof-of-work when the dispute is later challenged.

B. Confidence Ledger (VariantC) — risk-tone pills (Strong/Watch/Risk) on each leg in the Ready hero based on evidence density and instruction-override count. Tells operators where to focus their review instead of skimming everything equally. Cheap to wire if we already track evidence counts and instruction overrides.

C. V4 leg chips with embedded status — replace the current leg selector dropdown with horizontal chips that always show Ready / Needs walk / In progress / Hold so operators can scan group health without opening a menu. The most "free" UX win in the bunch.

D. V5 color-coded footer per phase — the footer changes color (blue for Re-attest, amber for Close, green for Submit) based on what action is required. Makes the "what does this group need from me right now?" question answerable from peripheral vision.

E. Q4–Q7 explicit off-ramps — instead of the binary "Queue for portal" CTA, surface Submit / Re-attest / Close as three first-class paths with reason selectors. Currently Re-attest and Close-out are buried behind context menus, so operators default to Submit even when it's wrong.

4. Smaller visual / friction polish
Step-breadcrumb pills (R-series) in the walk player — clickable answered-question chips above the current question, so the operator can jump back without opening the rewind dialog. Strictly better than what we shipped today; today's flow assumes the operator wants the "are you sure" friction; the breadcrumb assumes they don't.
Resolve → Next leg auto-focus: today there's a click between "Resolved leg 3" and "start leg 4". Make it one click with a 2-second undo banner, like Gmail's archive.
Gauntlet stepper consolidation: today MiniPhase appears in both the footer and the GauntletHero — two sources of progress truth. Pick one (footer) and remove the other.
V3 LandingStartWalk — first-impression of an unstarted group is a wall of UI. The mockup drew a focused "Start walking" splash with the leg count and ETA. Worth lifting for the empty/cold-start state.
