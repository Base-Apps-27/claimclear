Sharp edges (preserve when continuing)
phase_entered_at is the migration timestamp on every prod row — don't surface as "time entered phase".
Don't replicate B-PR2's claim.status deriver dependency in readers — read disposition from the column.
GROUP_SUBMITTED_STUCK_STATUSES Portal Queued asymmetry: post-submit at group level, pre-submit at claim level.
SSE emit sites can opportunistically populate optional phase? / disposition? fields (free in Wave C).
Trigger is DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED — fires at COMMIT. Each test statement is its own auto-commit, so it fires immediately in tests.
Closed phase has no non-terminal leg. Only final_reattested and final_no_action are valid for closed-phase children. If a test seeds a closed group, the child claim WILL be frozen — there's no way around it.
Dashboard aggregates are not all phase-swappable. Many KPIs in dashboard.ts are inherently status-based (e.g. groupBy(claimsTable.status) for status-breakdown charts). Only swap phase-membership predicates, not response-shape aggregates. When in doubt, leave it status-based and add a TODO for Wave D.
lib/leg-state/src/per-leg-sub-status.ts deliberately skips disposition === 'unclassified' to allow the legacy ladder to fall through during Wave C. Don't change this until Wave D backfills dispositions.
