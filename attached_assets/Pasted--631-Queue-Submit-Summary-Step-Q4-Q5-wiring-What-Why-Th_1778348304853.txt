#631 - Queue Submit Summary Step (Q4 → Q5 wiring)
What & Why
The end-of-walk wizard inside the Queue side pane is broken in a specific way that makes the final submit step unreachable. This task aligns the in-pane mini-wizard with the Canvas Q4 (Review) and Q5 (Submit) mockups — without leaving the side pane, without adding any new routes, without changing the hero state machine.

The exact problem (in code terms)
The gauntlet body (invoice-group-submission-gauntlet.tsx) renders both a Mark reviewed button at lines 574–591 and a Submit to portal button at lines 660–694, both buried inside the Review & edit panel.
The pinned footer's mini-submit-cta (inline-group-workspace-mini.tsx:1730–1742) is hidden whenever hero === "generate" | "review" | "ready" (line 464: hideSubmit={...}) precisely to avoid duplicating the gauntlet's body submit button.
When the operator clicks Mark Reviewed:
useMarkInvoiceGroupDraftReviewed runs.
hero flips from review → ready because previewGenerated && draftReviewed (line 353).
The gauntlet (and its body Submit button) is unmounted; only <ReadyHero /> (a near-empty placeholder) renders.
The footer Submit is still hidden because hideSubmit covers ready too.
Result: no working submit affordance anywhere on screen.
What it should be (from Q4/Q5 mockups)
Q4 (Review) = current hero === "review". The body is for editing only (subject + body textarea). The forward action Mark reviewed lives in the pinned footer as the primary CTA (Q4's FooterReview shows Continue to submit as the primary footer button — in our app the equivalent action is "Mark reviewed", since marking reviewed is what advances to Submit). The footer also carries: a status pill (Note ready when clean, Unsaved edits when dirty), a helper line, the Walk ✓ · Generate ✓ · Review & edit · Submit progress chips, and a Discard edits secondary button (revert local subject/body to the saved server values; only enabled when dirty).
Q5 (Submit) = current hero === "ready". The body is the summary view (last-check banner, destination header, compact legs, locked final note, attachments-by-leg). The forward action Queue for Portal lives in the pinned footer as the primary CTA. The footer also carries: Ready to send pill, the signing meta (reviewer name + timestamp + bot icon, right-aligned), and a Back to review secondary button that flips the hero back to review.
Why this stays small
No hero state additions: we keep review and ready exactly as they are derived today (line 353–354).
No new mutations, no new routes: Mark Reviewed wires to the existing useMarkInvoiceGroupDraftReviewed; Queue for Portal wires to the existing useCreatePortalSubmission.
No new gates: the existing 6-condition gate in PinnedFooter (lines 1648–1666) is reused verbatim for Queue for Portal. The Mark reviewed footer button reuses the existing in-body button's enable conditions.
The gauntlet's in-body Mark reviewed (lines 574–591) and Submit-to-portal (lines 660–694) sections are removed; the footer takes over for both.
hideSubmit for review and ready is replaced with footer rendering of the right CTA per hero.
